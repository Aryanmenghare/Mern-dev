PORSCHE

Misson S

The Porsche Mission S (often searched as "Vision S") is a mid-engined super sports car vision model unveiled on October 15, 2026, in Queens, New York.
Positioned conceptually above the 911, it previews Porsche's next design direction and a new mid-engine sports car platform under its "Sportwagenschmiede '35" realignment

Key Milestones & Development Context

  1. Strategic Alignment. The concept was developed as a core pillar of Porsche’s 'Sportwagenschmiede '35' strategic realignment to define its future sports car portfolio.
  2. Historic Location Selection. Porsche chose a pier in Queens, New York, for the reveal—the exact site where iconic photos of the mid-engined 550 Spyder were taken in 1953.
  3. Official World Premiere. The vehicle was officially unveiled on October 15, 2026, as detailed by the.
  4. Platform Positioning. Rather than a limited-edition hypercar, the Mission S previews a permanently available mid-engined super sports car sitting above the sports car lineup.
  5. Future Production. While production specifications, exact pricing, and launch dates remain unannounced, Porsche confirmed the model's design direction will soon translate into future production vehicles.

Design & Feature Highlights

  • Exterior Styling. Features "floating volumes," torpedo fenders, an integrated active rear wing, and a continuous main line connecting the front and rear light signatures.
  • Heritage Cues. Draws inspiration from classic Porsche models, including legacy sports cars (round headlights), GT1 models (flat front), 959 (roofline), 917 (cooling details), and 928 (lightweight dial-style wheels).
  • Purist Cockpit. Eliminates a permanently integrated center infotainment screen in favor of a retractable MagSafe smartphone mount for navigation and media.
  • Driver-Focused Instrumentation. Centers around five round instrument tubes behind the steering wheel—featuring an analogue tachometer needle—paired with tactile push-pull controls.
